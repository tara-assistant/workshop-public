ATM Milan · diagnostics architecture

Monitoring that connects assets, signals, and decisions.

I designed diagnostics architecture spanning three metro lines and roughly 100 km, covering 120 trainsets, and managed a team of two engineers. The program recorded a sevenfold reduction in vibration-related rail and wheel damage events within one year. ATM Milan role concluded in September 2026.

A note on privacy

Clear boundaries.

The portfolio is static and has no private server keys. Some apps contact their map or data providers directly from your browser. If an app requires a paid API key, it should ask you to supply your own, and keep it in your browser. Read each app’s notice before use; external providers may receive ordinary requests such as map tiles or routes.
